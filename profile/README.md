# Lex Analytics Group

**Legal technology, data analytics and AI — _Tahlil, tajriba, xulosa._**

Lex Analytics Group is a legal-technology company based in Tashkent, Uzbekistan. We build
software that makes legal information searchable, analysable and actionable — for lawyers,
banks, compliance teams and the public.

## What we build

| Product | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **CaseAI** — [caseai.uz](https://caseai.uz) | AI-assisted legal research and case analysis |
| **Malaka Sertifikati** — [malakasertifikati.uz](https://malakasertifikati.uz) | Certification, credit tracking, exam simulation and verification |
| **Bankrotlik.uz** — [bankrotlik.uz](https://bankrotlik.uz) | Insolvency and bankruptcy information platform |
| **Bank Yuristi** — [bankyuristi.uz](https://bankyuristi.uz) | Legal tooling for banking and finance |
